Stability Is Harder Than Change

At least for blogging. My daily life is pretty boring - work, learn something, blog, play games, watch anime, read, sleep. Not exactly catalysts for blogging. It was easier to find topics to talk about every day when I needed to actively look for inspirations and motivation.

Which is why I want to start broadening my topics, not just write about whatever random though pops into my head in the evenings. Maybe I'll even start a list of prompts for myself to choose from when inspiration is otherwise lacking.

I've also been thinking of more styles of writing, such as poetry or short stories, but I think I'd put those somewhere other than the blog section. Maybe they'd fit into art somewhere.

Otherwise the only thing on my mind right now is how lethargic my body feels from following my current 5k training plan.
