Bearable Weather

The entire weekend's weather was bearable. It's been months since this has happened! I found myself going outside 3 whole times today. That's unheard of. Sweating and being exhausted from the heat is a once-a-day sort of deal.

I'm so ready for long weekend bike rides in my near future. My run today was the longest continuous training I've done in the current plan, and yet I felt like I was keeping pace easily enough and felt like I would have been fine to keep going even after my timer marked the end of the training. I wonder if the humidity has reduced too, because until now sweat would not evaporate. It makes a huge difference in keeping cool.

Rounding out the nice weather is the bright full moon that's been out in the clear skies for the past couple of days. It's beautiful, as always.
