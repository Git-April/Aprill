Finishing the Season

Attack On Titan. I'm at a loss for words. So much feeling.

Definitely a series that I'm going to have to watch again, but I think this has got to be the one that's going to be the toughest to rewatch while knowing the outcome. The story is going to feel so much different the second time through. It's also going to be more clear in the moment, what's happening, and I bet I'll pick up on a few little details I missed.

After recovering and processing, of course I've also been looking up fan art of my favorite character. I've got a new phone lock screen for the first time in a while.
