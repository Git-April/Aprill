Reading Progress

Over the past week or so, I've felt like I've been making good progress on many of the books I have in progress, so I thought I'd record the books I'm reading here.

On Libby1, I currently have a fiction about an assassin and a manga in progress.

I also have a PDF book on the countries of Africa in progress as part of a free Coursera course I found.

Then, in terms of physical books, I have a fiction that I found in a Little Free Library2, a manga book from the series I bought, and a book about Japanese poetry.

Finally, not exactly book-books, but I've been counting my coloring book and my Book of Good Times3 while looping between a book and a screen activity.

Just a few days of trying to read a chapter of each every day results in real, visible progress where progress otherwise felt impossible.

I used to be a one-book-at-a-time person, but this new pattern works well for me too. It helps that each book is very different from the others.
