Listening

On starting my evening, I felt weird and overstimulated and worked up. The fire alarms had been going off all day for testing and they were so loud, I think it threw me off balance.

The cool thing is that I recognized this instead of trying to push through with a usual routine. I asked myself what I needed, paid attention to what my mind and body were feeling, and started out with a nice relaxing bubble batch with meditation music playing. I needed to let my whole system relax and calm down. Then I read a book for a while, escaping to a different world to reset without overstimulating social media, games, or videos. All of this combined has me feeling more balanced now.

Daily blogging is making healthier habits stick. Putting thoughts into words gives them a more permanent place in my brain even if I don't reread my posts often.
