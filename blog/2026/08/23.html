Gratitude in the Moment

I've been noticing small moments of gratitude lately the moment things happen.

The sky turned a really pretty orange and pink color at sunset today.

The weather was cloudy, but not raining, when I was travelling by bicycle, which kept the weather cooler and the sun out of my eyes.

There were people already at pottery when I arrived. I found a plate that had gone missing on the shelves.

The ice cream cones I bought were exactly the type I was hoping for, with the peanuts on top of the chocolate coating.

When my heart was going too hard and fast while biking, I was grateful that my recent exercize was helping me to handle it.

This might be referred to as savoring rather than gratitude. It's not something that I'm used to experiencing in the past. I'm not bringing it through intentionally, so I'm not sure what has triggered this.
