Gardening Terms

  • Cloud: Blocker of a clear perspective, they gather and enclose around as fog over time when a stagnant routine takes hold. Can be cleared by Wandering.
  • Fertilizer: External inspiration.
  • Garden: General term encompassing the entire set. Careful, you might start thinking of yourself as a garden - the accumulation of everything that is the self - past, present, and future. Positive existential crisis, anyone? :)
  • Path: The outcome of effort, whether small and overgrown or large and clear.
  • Rainbow: Flow1 state, or any positive-mood chemicals resulting from effort.
  • Seed: Fresh, new, hardly-formed idea.
  • Tool: Skill of any level of proficiency.
  • Toolshed: Set of tools available for use in the garden. Tools may be underdeveloped or rusty; consider maintenance before use.
  • Wandering: A trip through the garden to assess what to work on next and evaluate pathbuilding.
  • Water: Effort.
  • Weed: Bug or sub-obtimal code (such as duplication).
